# 🚀 VOID SURVIVORS – Update #3 (v1.5.4)
The Mothership has learned to dogfight – and space just got solid. 🛸

**⚔️ Gameplay**
• **The Mothership flies like a fighter jet** – banking hard as it circles you, Immelmanns, Split-S turns, barrel rolls, yo-yos, decoy flares and boom-and-zoom dives from above.
• It fires on the move: wingtip salvos aimed where you're heading, spinning laser spirals and double/triple laser rings.
• Nastier attacks: strafing runs down a red lane, real bombing flyovers and one smooth rift-jump dive-bomb.
• **New: Encirclement** – a ring of bombs with one gap. Find it. **New: Cloak** – it vanishes, stalks you unseen and strikes from right above.
• Last Stand: no more tractor beam – it goes berserk instead.
• **No more flying through space objects!** Stars scorch you, Saturn and the Dead World bounce you off, the Magnetar shocks you, the White Hole shoots you out at huge speed. Enemies still pass through – only you have to steer.
• The Observer's three specials are here: Tentacles from the Deep, Lash and Gaze of the Void.

**🎨 Visuals**
• **Brand-new Mothership** – a huge void manta with a spear-like nose, swept sickle wings, pulsing violet light strips and ghostly afterimages.
• **Seraphiel reborn** – new model with gold-traced armour and real feathered wings, a reworked scythe, true scythe-fighter swings and bird-like flight.
• **Lone Wanderer reborn** – plated armour, glowing turquoise crystals and orbit chains.
• Heroes keep their true colours from every angle and stay crisp in the horde.
• The creepier Observer: writhing tentacles, a twitching pupil and a bloodshot, weeping eye.

**🐛 Fixes**
• More translation fixes – dash key, skill tree bonuses, Trees perks, Bestiary and Star Forge no longer show Czech words.

### 🐛 Fixes
- The dash key in the ability readout (top left) now shows in your language – e.g. "[SPACE]" instead of the Czech "[MEZERNÍK]".
- More translation fixes: the bonus lines in the skill trees (e.g. "Cooldown: chain lightning −15 %"), the perk list on the Trees screen, "undiscovered" in the Bestiary and "MAXIMUM" in the Star Forge shop no longer show Czech words in English, German, Spanish or Russian.

### 🌑 Gameplay
- The Mothership now flies like a fighter jet: always nose-first, easing into and banking hard through its turns as it circles you at high speed – slowing in tight turns, speeding up on the straights, rising and dipping as it flies. Every few seconds it peels away, swings round and swoops low past right beside you. It only hurts you with its shots and with attacks marked on the ground.
- The Mothership flies real fighter-pilot manoeuvres: it turns around with an Immelmann (half loop up, half roll) or a Split-S (half roll, half loop down), does a barrel roll and jinks from side to side as it screams past you, climbs into a high yo-yo when it's about to overshoot and dives into a low yo-yo to catch up, and when you hit it hard it breaks away in a hard turn, firing decoy flares. Between ramming and bombing runs it zooms up and comes back down at you from above (boom and zoom).
- The Mothership fires on the move: salvos from its wingtips aimed where you're heading, spinning laser spirals from its belly, and double or triple rings of lasers.
- Mothership attacks got nastier: the ram is now a series of strafing runs (it pulls away to build up a run, turns its nose on you, a red lane lights up, it blasts down it spraying lasers from its wingtips to both sides, then loops back for more), the bombing run is a real flyover – you can see the bombs fall from its belly exactly where it passes (twice from phase 2) – and the rift jump is one smooth dive: it flies straight into a rift, bursts out of another, dive-bombs you and skims on past (from phase 2 the impact also sends out a ring of lasers).
- New Mothership attack – Encirclement: it whips around you in a tight circle, drops a ring of bombs with one gap, then strikes the centre. Get out through the gap!
- New Mothership attack – Cloak: the ship fades out until only a faint ripple in the air betrays it, stalks around you unseen, then a red circle marks the ground under you and it decloaks right above you and strikes. From phase 2 it strikes twice in a row.
- Mothership Last Stand: no more tractor beam – it goes berserk instead, flying faster, firing and attacking more often.
- No more flying through space objects! Stars, planets, the Station Wreck, asteroids, the Magnetar, the Comet and the Dead Leviathan's skull and spine are now solid – and crashing into them has consequences:
  - Stars (the Red Sun, the Dying Star and the star that lives out its whole life) scorch you: a heavy hit, you keep burning for a few seconds and the heat throws you back. "TOO CLOSE TO THE STAR!" flashes on screen when you get near.
  - Saturn and the Dead World bounce you off with a jolt and a bit of damage. Asteroids knock you back.
  - The Station Wreck is a metal wall – just bump into it to grab its loot.
  - Touch the Magnetar's core and an electric shock hurls you away.
  - Fly into the heart of the White Hole and it shoots you out at huge speed, spitting out crystals.
  - The Comet's core knocks you aside – you still get its speed boost.
  - Enemies can still pass straight through them – only you have to steer around.

### 🎨 Visuals
- Brand-new Mothership: a huge alien manta ship of dark brushed-metal armour plates with worn edges and rivets, catching the starlight as it banks. Violet light strips sit deep in grooves along its wings and spine with pulses of light flowing through them, the cockpit is a dome of black glass, its engine flares and spits a longer flame when it boosts, strobe lights blink on its wingtips, glowing trails stream from its wingtips, and it leaves violet ghostly afterimages when it speeds past.
- The odd beam of light under the Mothership is gone.
- Heroes glow-up: Seraphiel, the Lone Wanderer and Victor Hale now keep their true colours from every angle – no more fading into a dark smudge whenever they turn away from the starlight. They have more body from the side, their outlines stay crisp in the middle of the horde instead of shimmering, and a soft rim of light makes them easy to spot.
- Seraphiel reborn: the fallen angel has a brand-new body – white armour cracked with glowing gold, a black battle suit, a hood and a long tattered cloak – and brand-new wings made of real layered feathers: long ivory flight feathers edged in gold, with gold veins that glow. The wings still fold, flare open, ripple and shed feathers in flight, and the scythe is still in Seraphiel's hand.

### 🐛 Fixes
- Titan phase announcements (Leviathan, the Observer, the Star Devourer) now show in your language instead of Czech.
- Elite enemy tags (Explosive, Splitting, Phantom, Healing, Hastening, Giant) now show in your language instead of Czech – in combat and in the Bestiary.

### 🌑 Gameplay
- The Mothership now bursts out of a huge slanted rift, climbs high and crashes down on you – get out of the red circle.
- New Mothership attacks: a ramming charge down a red lane, a carpet-bombing run, and a rift jump that ends in another crash landing.
- Gravity slingshot: swing sideways past a massive body (black hole, stars, Saturn, the Quasar...) and its gravity flings you up to 2.6x faster, so you can race across the map. The boost fades over about half a minute.
- The horde keeps up: enemies speed up when you slingshot, and stragglers left far behind re-emerge ahead of you.
- New small speed readout in km/s under the kill counter (can be turned off in Graphics settings).
- Enemies hit harder and take more punishment: +50% health (and it grows faster as the run goes on), +30% damage from touch and projectiles. Bosses have +25% health.
- Health, healing and lifesteal cards now show up about three times less often – survival has to be earned.
- The Dying Star now lives its whole life, over and over: a healing protostar, a blue supergiant that burns enemies, a swelling red supergiant that burns you too, an unstable star hurling flares into red circles – then the 9-second countdown, the supernova, and a glowing remnant full of crystals from which the next star is born. The edge-of-screen marker tells you which phase it is in.
- The Observer has three new special attacks, each marked on the ground first: Tentacles from the Deep (rows of red circles race toward you and a tentacle bursts out of each one), Lash (it rears back one of its own tentacles and slams it down a red strip) and Gaze of the Void (the eye shuts, then a red wave rolls out from it – only the bright gaps are safe). It never uses the same special twice in a row.
- The Mothership now moves like a real UFO: it hangs in place, then darts to a new spot with no inertia and stops dead, its hull rocking from the sudden halt. Sometimes it phases out in glitching slices and reappears somewhere else around you. It fires its laser salvos and launches swarms only once it has stopped, and it can't hurt you by touch mid-dart. In its last stand it calms down and drags you in with its beam.

### 🎨 Visuals
- Robot invasion redesigned – the robots are now battered war machines: scorched bone-white armour with plates torn off, a black frame underneath and seams that glow red-hot.
- New robot shapes: the Drone is a bladed fighter with scythe wings, pincer jaws and a red triangular eye; the Spider has eight high hooked legs, fangs, a spiked glowing spine and a cluster of eyes; the Laser Warden is a horned sniper with claws, spikes and a long bayonet rifle.
- The Laser Warden now keeps its rifle aimed at you – its visor and muzzle light up right before it fires.
- The Mothership is now a long angular cruiser with jaw-like prongs on its bow, spines along the hull and red rings that stare up at you like slit-pupil eyes.
- Robots now move like real machines: Spiders rear up when they get close, Drones bank into turns, and Laser Wardens kick back after every shot.
- Laser Wardens paint a red targeting beam on you while they charge – you can see exactly who is about to fire.
- Robots show their damage: armour plates fall off and short-circuits flash across the hull. Destroyed robots keel over, glow red-hot and break apart.
- Sharper robot armour: bevelled plates, rivets, scratches, scorch marks and blood-red war paint – every robot is worn differently.
- The Mothership got engine flames and jaws that open when it releases a swarm, and it visibly falls apart as you tear it down. Robot explosions are now red instead of green.
- The invasion gate is no longer a green hexagon. Space now cracks and tears open into a burning red rift – and the robots fly out of it instead of just appearing.
- The Robot Invasion and Mothership warnings now glow red to match the robots.
- Mothership glow-up: waves of light race around a ring of lamps along its hull, its alien metal shimmers with an oily rainbow sheen, a scan line sweeps the hull, its engines flare when it darts, and it leaves red ghostly afterimages behind.
- Spiders and Laser Wardens no longer run across empty space – they fly on thrusters, legs trailing behind them.
- Enemies dragged into the Black Hole are now spaghettified – they stretch into glowing threads as they fall in. Shadows near the hole no longer vanish in the glare of its disc.
- The Observer is far creepier: long tentacles writhe around it and reach for you, its slit pupil twitches as it tracks you, its eyelid flinches, its flesh glistens with slime and a dark aura hangs behind it. In later phases the eye goes bloodshot and weeps blood. It now turns toward you, so you can actually look it in the eye.

## Update #1 – 14. 11. 2026 (v1.5.0) ✅ posted (pozdě: páteční automatický běh selhal na vyčerpaném limitu, posláno ručně v sobotu 01:19)

### ⚙️ New
- GRAPHICS settings menu – presets (Low → Ultra), render scale (FSR), anti-aliasing, shadows and FPS cap. Tune the game for your own PC.
- FPS counter – press F3 in game.

### 🚀 Performance
- Big optimization pass – smoother gameplay with 250+ enemies on screen.
- No more stutter when a titan spawns.
- Faster hero selection screen.
- Lower GPU usage in menus.
- FPS is now capped at 60 by default, so your PC stays smooth while you play (change it in GRAPHICS).

### 🌑 Gameplay
- "The Dark Closes In" now escalates – every phase brings more enemies, tougher enemy types, more HP and (from phase 4) a double ring.
- After minute 30 the dark no longer sends a ring – it summons one of the three great titans (Leviathan, the Observer or the Star Devourer), each one stronger than the last.

### ⚔️ Balance
- Bosses and titans are much tougher (around +60% HP) – Leviathan, the Eye and the Devourer won't melt anymore.

### 🎨 Visuals
- Space objects rebuilt: Saturn (storms, polar hexagon, detailed rings), the White Hole (matter fountain and spiral), the Dying Star (boiling surface), the Dead World (molten impact sea), the Nebula (filaments, dust lanes, newborn stars) and the Comet (glowing coma).
- Stars now erupt – flares burst across their surface and plasma arcs rise from the edge.
- Reworked Alien hero – less shiny, better proportions, smoother movement.
- Reworked Angel hero – better proportions, smoother wings and cloth.

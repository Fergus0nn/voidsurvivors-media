## Unreleased (Update #1 – pátek 13. 11. 2026, v1.5.0)

### ⚙️ New
- GRAPHICS settings menu – presets (Low → Ultra), render scale (FSR), anti-aliasing, shadows and FPS cap. Tune the game for your own PC.
- FPS counter – press F3 in game.

### 🚀 Performance
- Big optimization pass – smoother gameplay with 250+ enemies on screen.
- No more stutter when a titan spawns.
- Faster hero selection screen.
- Lower GPU usage in menus.
- FPS is now capped at 60 by default, so your PC stays smooth while you play (change it in GRAPHICS).

### 🌑 Gameplay
- "The Dark Closes In" now escalates – every phase brings more enemies, tougher enemy types, more HP and (from phase 4) a double ring.
- After minute 30 the dark no longer sends a ring – it summons one of the three great titans (Leviathan, the Observer or the Star Devourer), each one stronger than the last.

### ⚔️ Balance
- Bosses and titans are much tougher (around +60% HP) – Leviathan, the Eye and the Devourer won't melt anymore.

### 🎨 Visuals
- Space objects rebuilt: Saturn (storms, polar hexagon, detailed rings), the White Hole (matter fountain and spiral), the Dying Star (boiling surface), the Dead World (molten impact sea), the Nebula (filaments, dust lanes, newborn stars) and the Comet (glowing coma).
- Stars now erupt – flares burst across their surface and plasma arcs rise from the edge.
- Reworked Alien hero – less shiny, better proportions, smoother movement.
- Reworked Angel hero – better proportions, smoother wings and cloth.

### 🐛 Fixes
- Titan phase announcements (Leviathan, the Observer, the Star Devourer) now show in your language instead of Czech.
- Elite enemy tags (Explosive, Splitting, Phantom, Healing, Hastening, Giant) now show in your language instead of Czech – in combat and in the Bestiary.
